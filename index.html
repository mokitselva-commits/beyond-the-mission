Mission chronology

Footprints through time

A short timeline connecting the missions represented in this prototype.

1969 · MOON

Apollo 11 lands at Tranquility Base

The Lunar Module's descent stage remains on the lunar surface after the ascent stage departs.

1971 · MOON

Apollo 15 introduces the Lunar Roving Vehicle

The first lunar rover expands the astronauts' range across the Hadley–Apennine region.

1997 · MARS

Mars Pathfinder delivers Sojourner

NASA's first Mars rover demonstrates mobile surface exploration.

2004–2018 · MARS

Spirit and Opportunity explore Mars

The twin Mars Exploration Rovers greatly outlast their planned 90-sol primary missions.

2018–2022 · MARS

InSight listens to the interior of Mars

The stationary lander studies marsquakes and concludes its mission in December 2022.

2021 · MARS

Perseverance lands in Jezero Crater

Its entry, descent and landing system leaves a trail of hardware, including a parachute and backshell.

Why this archive exists

Not scrap. Evidence of exploration.

These objects are more than leftovers. They are physical records of engineering, risk, discovery and human curiosity. This prototype pairs concise storytelling with traceable sources so visitors can explore the facts behind each object.

Prototype status: This is a student-built demonstration. The location view is descriptive, not a precision landing-site map. No coordinates are invented. NASA image search is connected live where network access permits; if it is unavailable, the records remain readable with illustrated placeholders.
